# Research

My research focuses on noninvasive physiological speech interfaces and biomedical sensing.

## Physiological Speech Interfaces

Speech decoding and reconstruction from articulatory and neuromuscular physiological signals.

## Flexible Ultrasound

Flexible and wearable ultrasound technologies for tongue-motion sensing and speech decoding.

## Multimodal Speech Decoding

Fusion of surface electromyography (sEMG) and ultrasound tongue imaging (UTI) for speech reconstruction.

## Assistive Speech Technologies

Speech communication and rehabilitation technologies for individuals with speech impairments.
